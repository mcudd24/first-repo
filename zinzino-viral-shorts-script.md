# Zinzino Viral Short — Before/After Transformation Script

**Format:** YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels  
**Duration:** 45–60 seconds  
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical  
**Goal:** Drives curiosity → profile click → link in bio → zinzino.com

---

## HOOK (0:00–0:03) — Do NOT lose them here

**On screen (text overlay, bold, high contrast):**
> "I tested my blood and the results terrified me 😰"

**Visual:** Extreme close-up of a finger-prick blood test kit (Zinzino Test).  
**Creator action:** Staring at the result, shaking head slowly.  
**No voiceover yet** — silence or viral trending audio underneath.

> **Why it works:** Fear + curiosity + personal stakes = instant stop-scroll.

---

## BEFORE (0:03–0:12) — Establish the pain

**Voiceover / text captions (both):**
> "Six months ago I was exhausted every single day.  
> Brain fog. Joints aching. Mood all over the place.  
> Doctors said everything looked 'normal.'"

**Visuals (quick cuts, 1–2 sec each):**
- Creator looking tired, slouched at desk
- Bottle of over-the-counter pain relief
- Scrolling phone in bed at midnight (dark room)
- B-roll: unhealthy fast food, stressed face

**Text overlay:** `BEFORE` (red, top corner)

---

## DISCOVERY (0:12–0:22) — Introduce Zinzino naturally

**Voiceover:**
> "Then a friend sent me a Zinzino Balance Test.  
> It measures your Omega-6 to Omega-3 ratio.  
> Mine was 20:1. Optimal is 3:1."

**Visuals:**
- Hold up the Zinzino Test kit to camera (product close-up)
- Show the result card / app screenshot (blur personal details if needed)
- Reaction: eyes wide, jaw drop

**Text overlay:** `The average ratio in the West is 15:1 😳`

> **Why it works:** Specific numbers = credibility. The stat creates a shared "uh oh" moment.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Lighting** | Ring light or window light, clean background |
| **Captions** | Auto-caption ON (CapCut / Descript) — 85% of Shorts watched muted |
| **Music** | Trending audio from TikTok/Reels — use the same audio as the original viral video |
| **Pacing** | Cut every 1.5–2 sec in the montage section; never let a shot breathe too long |
| **Creator energy** | Slightly over-expressive — the camera flattens 20% of your energy |
| **Hook test** | Record 3 different hooks, A/B test which drives the most 3-second retention |
